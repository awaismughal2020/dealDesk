SAMPLE RFP: Tenant self-service portal
Issued by: Northfield Homes (FICTIONAL ORGANISATION, SAMPLE DATA)

1. Background
Northfield Homes is a fictional housing association managing around 4,000 homes. Tenants currently
report repairs by phone. We want a self-service web portal.

2. Requirements
- The supplier shall deliver a responsive tenant web portal accessible to WCAG 2.2 AA.
- The portal must let tenants report repairs with photos and track repair status.
- The portal must show each tenant's rent account balance and recent transactions.
- The supplier shall provide secure messaging between tenants and the housing team.
- The portal must integrate with our housing management system through its REST API.
- The supplier shall implement single sign-on for staff using our identity provider.
- The supplier must provide an admin console for staff to manage content and users.
- The supplier shall carry out user research and usability testing with tenants.

3. Timeline
We would like to start within one month and go live within four months.

4. Evaluation
Quality 60%, price 40%. Please include relevant experience.
